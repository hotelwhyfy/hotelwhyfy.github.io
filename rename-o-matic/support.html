Support

Stuck? Found a bug? Got an idea? Email ansanfilippo@gmail.com and include your macOS version and what you were renaming. We usually reply within a couple of days.

How do I rename a bunch of files?

Open a folder with ⌘O or drag it onto the window. Press ⌘K to add rules. The list shows each new name as you go. When it looks right, press ⌘↩.

Can I just type what I want?

Yes. Press ⌘K and type it in plain English, for example replace IMG with Photo and add a suffix _v2 or remove numbers from the start and title case. A card shows the rules it understood; press ↩ to add them. If part of the sentence doesn't make sense, it tells you which part and shows an example. Putting text in quotes ("like this") always works when a phrase is ambiguous. It all runs on your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere.

I made a mistake. Can I undo it?

Yes. Click Undo in the confirmation that appears after a rename, or choose Rename ▸ Undo Last Rename (⌥⌘Z). For an older rename, click History in the toolbar (or press ⇧⌘Y), select it and click Undo Rename. History is kept after you quit the app. Undo works as long as the renamed files are still where Rename-o-matic left them.

Why are some rows marked as conflicts?

Two items would end up with the same name, the new name is already taken in that folder, or the name isn't allowed. Conflicting items are skipped, so nothing is ever overwritten. Tweak the recipe until the warning goes away.

How do I add Rename-o-matic to Finder's right-click menu?

Open System Settings ▸ General ▸ Login Items & Extensions, click the ⓘ next to Finder, and turn on Rename-o-matic. Then right-click files or a folder and choose Rename… (the item names what it will open, for example “Rename 3 Items…”).

Why does the app ask for access to a folder?

Mac App Store apps are sandboxed. They can only touch folders you choose. Rename-o-matic remembers the folders you grant, so it only asks once.

Can I share my recipes?

Yes. Choose Rename ▸ Export Recipe as Preset… to save a .rompreset file. Double-click one to import it.

System requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
