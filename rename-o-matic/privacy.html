Privacy Policy

Short version: Rename-o-matic doesn't collect anything.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Rename-o-matic has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party SDKs, and it doesn't connect to the internet. Commands you type into the ⌘K palette are understood by a parser that runs on your Mac; they aren't sent anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

To do its job, the app keeps a few things in its private sandbox container on your Mac:

  • Your settings and saved recipes (presets).
  • Your rename history (old and new names, and the folder), so you can undo.
  • Bookmarks for the folders you've given it access to.

This never leaves your Mac. Delete the app and it goes with it. You can also remove entries anytime with Forget in the Rename History window.

Your files

Rename-o-matic reads file names and metadata, such as dates, photo EXIF and music tags, only to build new names. It only renames items you choose, in folders you've granted, and only when you press Rename.

The Finder extension

The optional Finder extension only adds a menu item that opens the app. It doesn't read or send anything.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy label first. Questions: ansanfilippo@gmail.com.
